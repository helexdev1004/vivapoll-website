How can we help?

Find answers to common questions or get in touch with our support team. We’re here to help you get the most out of VivaPoll.

We’re
always here
for you!

Every survey shows its reward before you start. Most surveys pay between €0.50 and €3.00, depending on how long they take and how specific the target group is. Longer or more specialised surveys usually pay more.

Your rewards are added to your VivaPoll balance. Once you reach the minimum payout amount, open the Payout section in the app, choose one of the methods available in your country and confirm your request.

Surveys come from market research companies and brands. Topics include everyday products, food and drink, technology, travel, entertainment, advertising and social questions. Your answers help companies improve their products and services.

Most surveys take between 5 and 20 minutes. The estimated length is always shown next to the reward, so you can pick surveys that fit the time you have.

Researchers look for specific groups of people. If your answers to the first questions show that you are not in the group they need, the survey ends early. This is normal and does not affect your account.

New surveys become available throughout the day. Complete your profile so we can match you with more surveys, and check back regularly. Availability can also depend on your country.

Share your personal referral link or code with friends. When they join VivaPoll with your link and start completing activities, you receive a referral bonus.

The bonus is added once your friend has created a genuine account and passed our quality checks. The exact conditions are shown in the Referral section of the app.

A referral only counts if your friend installs the app through your link or enters your code when signing up, is a new user and meets the referral conditions. Self-referrals and duplicate accounts are not rewarded.

The minimum payout depends on your country and the payout method you choose. You can always see the current amount in the Payout section of the app.

Available methods vary by country and may include bank transfer, online wallets and gift cards. The options for your country are listed in the app.

Most payouts are processed within a few business days. You can follow the status of every request in your account.

Some partners need time to confirm that an activity was completed correctly. Pending rewards are usually confirmed within a few days and then move to your available balance.
