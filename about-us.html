Who we are

About VivaPoll

VivaPoll is a rewards platform that gives you different ways to earn — from sharing your opinions through surveys to discovering and playing games.

The website is owned and operated by Main Frame Ltd.

Real people.
Real opinions.
A brighter tomorrow.

What we do

Companies VivaPoll You

We connect people with companies, researchers, app developers and game publishers that want to hear from real people, introduce their products to new audiences, and grow their communities.

In return for your time and participation, you earn rewards.

Earn your way

More than one way to earn

Everyone has different interests and different amounts of free time. That’s why VivaPoll isn’t limited to just one way of earning.

Take surveys

Share your thoughts about products, brands, services and everyday topics.

Your feedback helps companies and researchers understand what people really think and make better decisions.

Play games

Discover new games and earn rewards by reaching specific goals and milestones.

Whether you’re trying a game you’ve never played before or looking for something new to spend your free time on, gaming offers give you another way to earn while you play.

Discover new opportunities

VivaPoll can also bring you other offers and activities from our partners.

Each opportunity comes with its own requirements and reward, so you can choose what works best for you.

How it works

It’s simple.

Three steps from choosing an offer to getting your reward.

  1. 1

    Choose an offer

    Browse the surveys, games and other opportunities available to you.

  2. 2

    Complete the activity

    Answer a survey, play a game and reach its milestones, or complete another eligible offer.

  3. 3

    Earn rewards

    Once your activity is successfully completed and verified, you’ll receive your reward.

Redeem your rewards

Exchange your balance for one of the rewards available through VivaPoll.

You choose how you participate. There’s no requirement to complete every offer, and you can come back whenever you want to discover new opportunities.

It’s a fair question

Why do companies pay you?

The rewards you receive come from the companies and partners behind the activities available on VivaPoll.

  • Researchers need people to participate in surveys and provide genuine feedback. Game developers and app companies want to introduce their products to new users and grow their audiences.

  • VivaPoll helps connect these companies with people who are interested in participating.

  • When you successfully complete an eligible activity, the value generated from that activity is shared with you as a reward.

Your privacy matters

We know that earning through surveys and online activities involves sharing information, which is why transparency matters to us.

VivaPoll may use information about you to operate the platform, determine which opportunities may be relevant to you, verify participation, provide rewards, and meet our legal and contractual obligations.

Some activities are provided by third-party partners. When you participate in one of these activities, information may be shared with or processed by the relevant partner as necessary to provide and verify the activity.

We encourage you to understand how your information is used before participating.
